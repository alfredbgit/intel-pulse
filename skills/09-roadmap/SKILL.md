# Skill 09 — 30-Day Roadmap

## Purpose
Convert the Top 5 Findings into a practical, week-by-week action plan for the first 30 days.

## Inputs
- Skill 08 findings
- Customer's business type and resources
- Customer's current digital presence (from Skills 05, 06)

## Structure

### Week 1 — Foundation
Digital presence basics. Claim what's unclaimed. Do this first — it's the foundation everything else runs on.

### Week 2 — Review & Review Strategy
Get the customer's review engine running. Every positive review is a competitive advantage against chains with weak local presence.

### Week 3 — Differentiation
Use the findings to stand out. Pick the highest-impact action from the Top 5 Findings.

### Week 4 — Measure & Iterate
Don't just do — measure. Set up basic tracking so week 2 can be better than week 1.
